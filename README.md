# Awesome-Integrated-Business-Planning

## Top Integrated Business Planning (IBP) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on S&OP, Demand & Supply Planning, Scenario Planning, Concurrent Planning & Enterprise Planning Alignment*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Integrated Business Planning (IBP)**. These systems align demand, supply, inventory, finance, and operations in a single planning process—supporting S&OP, scenario analysis, and cross-functional decision-making.



**Examples** include SAP IBP, Kinaxis, Anaplan, Blue Yonder, OMP Unison Planning, Oracle IBP, Logility, ToolsGroup, RELEX Solutions, and John Galt Solutions (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise IBP suites are commercial. Practical open options include **frePPLe** (open supply chain planning), graph-based planning engines, and optimization libraries that teams can adapt for demand/supply planning. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the enterprise gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[SAP Integrated Business Planning (IBP)](https://www.sap.com/products/scm/integrated-business-planning.html)**  

  SAP’s cloud IBP solution tightly integrated with S/4HANA for demand, supply, inventory, and sales & operations planning.



- **[Kinaxis](https://www.kinaxis.com/)**  

  Concurrent planning platform known for rapid what-if scenario analysis and end-to-end supply chain orchestration (Maestro).



- **[Anaplan](https://www.anaplan.com/)**  

  Connected planning platform widely used for IBP, financial planning, and cross-functional scenario modeling.



- **[Blue Yonder](https://blueyonder.com/)**  

  Supply chain and retail planning suite covering demand, fulfillment, inventory, and integrated business planning use cases.



- **[OMP Unison Planning](https://www.omp.com/)**  

  Advanced planning platform focused on integrated and concurrent planning for complex manufacturing and supply networks.



- **[Oracle Integrated Business Planning / Oracle SCP](https://www.oracle.com/)**  

  Oracle’s supply chain and integrated planning capabilities within the Oracle Cloud SCM portfolio.



- **[Logility](https://www.logility.com/)**  

  Supply chain planning platform supporting demand, inventory, supply, and integrated business planning processes.



- **[ToolsGroup](https://www.toolsgroup.com/)**  

  Service-driven planning and inventory optimization platform often used as part of broader IBP programs.



- **[RELEX Solutions](https://www.relexsolutions.com/)**  

  Unified retail and supply chain planning platform strong in demand forecasting, replenishment, and integrated planning for retail.



- **[John Galt Solutions](https://johngalt.com/)**  

  Atlas Planning Platform supporting demand, supply, and integrated business planning for mid-market and enterprise organizations.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[frePPLe](https://github.com/frePPLe/frepple)**  

  Open-source demand forecasting and advanced planning & scheduling (APS) tool for manufacturing—community edition under MIT license.



- **[ootils-core and graph-based planning engines](https://github.com/ngoineau/ootils-core)**  

  Open-source, graph-based supply chain decision engine supporting propagation, shortage detection, MRP-style explosion, and scenario analysis.



- **[OR-Tools and open optimization solvers](https://github.com/google/or-tools)**  

  Google’s open optimization suite used to build custom production, inventory, and network planning models.



- **[PuLP / Pyomo open modeling frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Python-based open modeling languages for linear and mixed-integer planning and optimization problems.



- **[Time-series forecasting open libraries (Prophet, statsmodels, etc.)](https://github.com/)**  

  Open forecasting tools used as building blocks for demand planning components of IBP.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo manufacturing and planning modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open ERP modules supporting MRP, work orders, and basic supply planning that can feed higher-level IBP processes.



- **[Scenario and spreadsheet open collaboration tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open spreadsheet and notebook environments used for lightweight S&OP scenario modeling in smaller organizations.



- **[Inventory optimization open research projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Academic and community projects implementing safety stock, multi-echelon, and service-level optimization algorithms.



- **[Data pipeline open stacks for planning inputs](https://github.com/)**  

  Open ETL and lakehouse tools used to prepare clean demand, supply, and inventory data for planning engines.



- **[Dashboard open tools for S&OP visibility](https://github.com/)**  

  Grafana, Metabase, and similar open BI tools used to present planning KPIs and scenario comparisons.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **frePPLe** for manufacturing-centric demand and production planning where a full IBP suite is not required.

- Building custom planning logic with **graph engines + OR-Tools** for specialized network or constraint problems.

- Using open forecasting libraries as the demand engine inside a broader commercial or hybrid IBP process.

- Accepting that concurrent planning at enterprise scale, finance integration, multi-enterprise collaboration, and mature S&OP workflows still favor commercial platforms (SAP IBP, Kinaxis, Anaplan, Blue Yonder, OMP, Oracle, Logility, RELEX, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency of models, cost control, and specialized planning algorithms.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Forecast demand with open time-series tools → plan supply and capacity with frePPLe or optimization solvers → run scenarios in notebooks or graph engines → present results in open dashboards → align decisions in S&OP meetings. Suitable for mid-market manufacturers and teams with planning expertise. Most large enterprises adopt commercial IBP platforms for cross-functional scale and support.
